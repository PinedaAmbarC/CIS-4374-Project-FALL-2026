# Team Charter

**Project:** Smart Parking Platform (Version 6.0) **Date:** September 11, 2026

## 1\. Team Purpose and Mission

Our mission is to successfully design, develop, and deploy the Smart Parking Platform by December 18, 2026\. As a cross-functional team at \$oftware corp., we will collaborate to deliver a secure, high-performing application that reduces event traffic congestion, maximizes parking operator revenue, and provides a seamless mobile checkout experience for drivers.

## 3\. Team Norms and Communication

* **Daily Alignment:** The team will hold daily standup meetings to discuss progress, plan the day's work, and identify blockers (especially between frontend and backend integration).  
* **Task Management:** All project tasks, user stories, and bugs will be tracked on the team's **Trello Scrum Board**. We will utilize persona-based color-coded labels (e.g., Green for Mobile User, Blue for Operator, Red for Backend) to maintain visual organization.  
* **Quality First:** Quality Assurance is not just a final step; testing will be integrated into every project phase from design to deployment.  
* **Incident Response:** Automated alerts via **PagerDuty** will be used for critical technical issues (e.g., API latency \> 500ms). DevOps and Development will respond to these escalations promptly.

## 4\. Decision Making and Conflict Resolution

* **Consensus & Ownership:** Technical decisions will be driven by the Developers and DevOps, while product scope and user value decisions will be driven by the Product Owner.  
* **Conflict Resolution:** If a disagreement arises, the team will focus on data and SRS requirements to guide the solution. If an impasse is reached, the Project Manager (Alice S.) will facilitate a resolution, escalating to the Champion (Product Owner) if necessary.

## 5\. Success Criteria

* **On-Time Delivery:** Production launch and App Store/Play Store release by the target deadline of December 18, 2026\.  
* **Budget Adherence:** Keep total project costs, including infrastructure and labor, within the approved budget of \$232,320.  
* **Technical Excellence:** Meet all Quality of Service (QoS) metrics, notably keeping checkout API latency under 500ms during peak load tests.  
* **Stakeholder Satisfaction:** Achieve formal UAT sign-off from key business stakeholders and ensure all mitigation strategies for identified project risks are actively managed.
